# Prompts

These are the prompts for this redesign. The implementer follows the build prompt. The critic prompt is reused unchanged and does not contain a target score.

## Discover — living memorial brief

Build the Shri Prasanna Pattnaik Memorial Trust site as a living memorial, not a startup landing page. Keep saffron #C75B12, green #2E7D32, gold #B8860B, cream #FFFBF0, and ink #263238. Poppins for interface text. Newsreader only for the name, dates, and quotations. The Brahmani bridge fills the first screen. The trust name sits on it like an inscription. Section 80G is one sentence and one Donate action. Five focus areas are a photographic index with no emoji. Interior pages share one left-aligned masthead. Do not invent quotations, statistics, trustee portraits, or beneficiary photographs.

## Define — critic

You are seeing screenshots only. You do not know the code or earlier scores. Name the aesthetic the page is attempting. Say how a top studio would execute a memorial institution in this palette. List the biggest gaps in composition, type, color discipline, photography, restraint, continuity, and whether a visitor can read the life, find a program, and donate. Penalize glass, emoji, fake numbers, gradient chrome, and off-palette color. Be specific. Score each of those dimensions out of 10.

## Deliver — subtraction and copy

Remove anything that does not help a visitor trust the institution, read the life, find a program, or donate. Do not rewrite biography, registration facts, 80G wording, bank details, or the Google confirmation form.
